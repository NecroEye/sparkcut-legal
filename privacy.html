Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

1. Overview

Local-first processing

SparkCut respects your privacy. All video editing operations are performed entirely on your device. We do not upload, process, or store your media files on any external server.

2. Information We Collect

We collect minimal, anonymized data solely to improve the App experience:

Data Type Purpose Identifier
Crash reports Bug fixing Anonymous
Usage analytics Feature improvement Anonymous
Device model & OS Compatibility Anonymous
Ad interactions Ad relevance Ad ID (resettable)
Purchase history Subscription management & entitlements Anonymous app ID / Play order ID

3. Information We Do NOT Collect

Never collected
  • Your videos, photos, or audio files
  • Microphone or screen-recording content — recordings are saved only to your device
  • Your contacts, messages, or call history
  • Your precise or approximate location
  • Personally identifiable information (name, email, phone)
  • Browsing history or app usage outside SparkCut

4. Third-Party Services

SparkCut integrates the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

Service providers
  • Firebase Crashlytics — anonymous crash reporting
  • Firebase Analytics — anonymous usage statistics
  • Google AdMob — advertisement delivery
  • RevenueCat — subscription management and receipt validation

Google's advertising partners may use cookies or advertising identifiers to serve personalized ads. You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization).

5. Advertising

SparkCut displays advertisements through Google AdMob. These ads may use your device's Advertising ID to deliver relevant content. The Advertising ID is a resettable identifier and does not expose personal information.

In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, SparkCut shows a consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) before any ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time; non-personalized ads are available if you decline.

We comply with Google's Advertising Policies and Privacy Policy.

6. Purchases & Subscriptions

SparkCut offers an optional subscription (SparkCut Pro) with weekly, monthly, and yearly plans. All payments are processed by Google Play Billing — we never see or store your payment card details.

To activate and manage your subscription, purchase receipts are validated by RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.), which receives an anonymous app-generated identifier and Google Play purchase tokens. No name, email, or payment information is shared with RevenueCat. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can cancel anytime in Google Play > Subscriptions. Refunds are handled according to Google Play's refund policy.

7. Data Storage & Security

All your projects, exports, and media remain stored locally on your device. We do not have access to your device storage. Analytics data is transmitted securely via HTTPS to Firebase servers.

When you uninstall SparkCut, all local app data is permanently removed from your device.

8. Opt Out

You have full control over data collection:

  • Analytics: Disable in SparkCut Settings at any time
  • Personalized ads: Opt out via device settings
  • Crash reports: Disable in device system settings

9. Children's Privacy

SparkCut is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately and we will take steps to remove such information.

10. International Data Transfers

Anonymous analytics and crash data may be processed on servers located outside your country of residence. By using SparkCut, you consent to this transfer. All transfers are conducted securely and in compliance with applicable data protection laws.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be communicated through App updates. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect the most recent revision. Continued use of SparkCut constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to any data we hold about you
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to data processing
  • Lodge a complaint with a data protection authority

Since we do not collect personally identifiable information, most of these rights are satisfied by default.
